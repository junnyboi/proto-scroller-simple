# Act 1 Boss Music Provenance

The Settlement Engine instrumental theme was generated with **Lyria 3 Pro** on 2026-08-26 from an original Business district brief. No artist, song, album, copyrighted melody, lyrics, or vocal samples were referenced. Raw generations are external authoring material and are not shipped.

| Boss | Runtime file | Musical identity |
|---|---|---|
| SETTLEMENT ENGINE S-04 | `settlement-engine-s04.ogg` | 118 BPM industrial accounting machinery in D minor |

The runtime theme is loudness-normalized to approximately **-16 LUFS**, mono at **32 kHz**, and encoded as compact Ogg Vorbis at approximately **40 kbps**. Its arrangement returns toward its opening rhythmic and harmonic state for looped playback. `BossMusicDirector` owns one prewarmed AudioStreamPlayer on the Music bus. The theme begins at the first boss encounter and continues through defeat and salvage checkout. Actual run stop, extraction, defeat, title return, or a fresh run stops the boss player and restores the interrupted city-pressure bed.

The published theme is 216,798 bytes; its byte-exact CDN mapping is recorded in `assets.lock.json`. Release size is measured by `tools/verify.py`, rather than an allocation inherited from a different asset revision.
